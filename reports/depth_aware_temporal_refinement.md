# Depth-Aware Temporal Refinement: Preliminary Study

## Purpose

This document preserves the first phase of the project and explains why the research moved from depth-aware post-processing to removal-mask refinement.

The initial question was whether pseudo-depth could identify structurally unreliable regions and guide temporal stabilization around object-mask boundaries. The experiment used:

- ProPainter outputs as the video-inpainting baseline;
- Depth Anything 3 pseudo-depth maps;
- optical-flow warping of the previous refined frame;
- boundary, RGB-consistency, depth-consistency, and depth-edge-preservation weights.

The depth cue was designed as a conservative reliability gate. It was not treated as ground-truth geometry or as the sole cause of flicker.

## Controlled comparison

The main comparison used 20 AI-Hub clips. Boundary temporal error (BTE) measures flow-aligned frame-to-frame differences inside the mask-boundary band. `B&D` restricts a metric to mask-boundary pixels that also overlap a thresholded depth edge. Boundary preservation metrics use the ProPainter output as the reference because the tested methods are post-processing variants.

| Method | Boundary TE ↓ | BTE reduction vs baseline | B&D TE ↓ | B&D boundary F-score ↑ | B&D Chamfer ↓ | B&D change ↓ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ProPainter baseline | 0.018322 | 0.00% | 0.021109 | 1.0000 | 0.0000 | 0.000000 |
| Depth-aware refinement | 0.014628 | 20.16% | 0.018435 | 0.9786 | 5.6554 | 0.003118 |
| Boundary-only smoothing | **0.008634** | **52.88%** | **0.009972** | 0.9414 | 11.5339 | 0.013151 |

Frame-pair comparisons further separate temporal smoothing from structural preservation:

| Comparison | Frames | Ratio |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depth-aware has lower BTE than boundary-only | 44/776 | 5.67% |
| Depth-aware has better B&D boundary F-score | 593/776 | 76.42% |
| Depth-aware has lower B&D boundary change | 772/776 | 99.48% |

These results show a clear trade-off. Boundary-only smoothing reduces flicker more aggressively, while depth-aware refinement modifies depth-discontinuity boundaries less and better preserves their contours.

## Depth–temporal overlap probe

Before the 20-clip comparison, a three-clip probe measured overlap among the mask-boundary band (`B`), thresholded depth edges (`D`), and high temporal-error pixels (`T`).

| Metric | Mean |
|---|---:|
| `B & D / B` | 15.26% |
| `B & T / B` | 19.83% |
| `D & T / D` | 10.43% |
| `B & D & T / B` | 2.89% |

The strongest relationship was between the mask boundary and temporal error. The localized three-way overlap does not support treating depth discontinuities as a broad explanation for flicker.

## Research decision

The depth-aware method was not a null result: it improved BTE over ProPainter and provided a useful structural-preservation mechanism. However, the ablation showed that its temporal-error improvement was weaker than simple ungated smoothing. Post-processing also could not correct an input mask that omitted part of the object's visual support.

The project therefore moved upstream:

1. from post-inpainting depth-aware boundary stabilization;
2. to temporal occupancy and selective expansion of the input removal mask;
3. to budget-matched geometric controls and synthetic clean-background evaluation;
4. to the final finding that local boundary under-coverage, rather than a purely temporal failure mode, dominates the current synthetic-SAM setting.

This transition is the central research contribution of the preliminary phase: depth remained a plausible auxiliary cue, but controlled baselines prevented it from being presented as the primary solution.
